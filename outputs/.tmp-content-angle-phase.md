# Content Angle Plan — Phase 3 (grounded in business-brief.md + Phase 2 offer architecture only)

Methodology note: every substantive claim below is labeled **(Evidence — brief, line X)** with quote/paraphrase, or **(Inference)** with reasoning shown. No external platform research, no fabricated engagement data, no invented content slots. Per the task's hard override, this phase does **not** use a YouTube title/thumbnail/retention-map structure — it uses Reels/carousel hook lines, on-screen text, and a weekly angle plan, because Susu's platforms are Instagram and TikTok **(Evidence — brief, line 6)** and her format is fixed weekly slots, not standalone long-form video **(Evidence — brief, line 21, 18)**.

Scope discipline: this plan touches only the four existing touchpoints named in the brief — Tue outfit Reel, Wed styling carousel, Sat outfit/lifestyle Reel, Sun rest/review slot — plus how Braise Beauty BTS content should be angled within its own pillar. **(Evidence — brief, lines 18, 21, 40; Phase 2 handoff note)**. No fifth slot, no dedicated affiliate or Braise Beauty promo day is proposed, per Phase 1 and Phase 2's explicit instruction to optimize existing real estate.

## Voice Guardrails Applied to All Copy Below

Every hook/on-screen line in this document was written and checked against:
- No discount-code/haul/"finds under R300" language — reframed as "pieces I keep reaching for" / "what's in rotation." **(Evidence — brief, line 34)**
- Link mentioned at most once in caption, never in the hook itself, lives in bio/stories. **(Evidence — brief, line 35)**
- Her face is always the visual background — no plain colour slides, no generic text carousels. **(Evidence — brief, line 36)**
- Centred text only, thin serif font, no border frames, thin divider lines only. **(Evidence — brief, line 37)**
- No claims of results, no "this is working," no growth/sales framing before 90 days; save/share language, not like-bait. **(Evidence — brief, line 39)**
- Affiliate tracking/recap folded into the existing Sunday slot only — never a separate affiliate day. **(Evidence — brief, line 40)**
- No burnout/escape/struggle framing; "I love my 9–5 AND I'm building something more," "I'm not escaping — I'm expanding," "single motherhood didn't break me." **(Evidence — brief, lines 27–31)**
- Elevated, refined, luxury-adjacent, honest, documentary/personal tone — never generic-motivational, never AI-template-sounding. **(Evidence — brief, lines 24, 41)**

All specific hook copy below is proposed content, not brief-stated text, and is therefore labeled **(Inference)** as a set, with the reasoning for each shown individually.

## Weekly Touchpoint Angle Plan

### Tuesday — Outfit Reel (affiliate embedded, Fashion & Outfits pillar)

**Function per brief:** part of the existing affiliate-integrated schedule; affiliate lives inside the outfit content, never as its own promo lane. **(Evidence — brief, lines 18, 21)**

**Angle:** the outfit is evidence of a decision-making system, not a haul. The "systems mindset" (automation engineer applying engineering logic to style) is the differentiator — **(Evidence — brief, line 24)**: "Applies an engineer's systems mindset to beauty, style, and life."

**Representative hook (on-screen text, first frame, her face as background):**
> "What I put on when I need to think clearly."
**(Inference)**: reframes an outfit Reel away from "get this look" (haul energy, banned by line 34) toward identity/function — consistent with "when you look good, you believe bigger" **(Evidence — brief, line 10)**.

**Caption direction (link mentioned at most once, near end):**
> "Not a trend — a system. This blazer's been in rotation for three weeks and I keep reaching for it. Everything's linked in bio, if you want it." **(Evidence — brief, lines 34, 35)** applied directly.

### Wednesday — Styling Carousel (affiliate embedded, Fashion & Outfits pillar)

**Function per brief:** carousel format, same pillar as Tuesday, must never read as a "shop this" post. **(Evidence — brief, lines 18, 21, 34, 36, 37)**

**Angle:** carousel as a documented styling *decision tree* — showing the "why," not a product grid. Each slide is her face styled differently, centred thin-serif caption text on top, thin divider line separating slides — never a text-only slide. **(Evidence — brief, lines 36, 37)**

**Representative hook (slide 1, on-screen text over her face):**
> "How I actually decide what goes together."
**(Inference)**: sets up a "systems" narrative (decision logic) rather than a "these are my picks" list, which the brief's haul ban implicitly rules out **(Evidence — brief, line 34)**.

**Slide progression (Inference, consistent with visual rules line 37):**
- Slide 2: "Start with the piece I don't want to retire."
- Slide 3: "Then build around what it needs, not what's trending."
- Slide 4: "This is what's in rotation this week." **(Evidence — brief, line 34)**: direct reuse of the brief's own sanctioned phrase.
- Final slide: single-line caption sign-off, no CTA stack, no border frame.

### Saturday — Outfit/Lifestyle Reel (affiliate embedded, Fashion & Outfits + Documentary crossover)

**Function per brief:** same affiliate-integrated schedule as Tue/Wed **(Evidence — brief, line 21)**, but "lifestyle" gives it room to cross into the Documentary pillar ("Building Her, Cape Town 2026") **(Evidence — brief, line 18)** — weekend content can carry more of the "engineered life" identity than the weekday outfit posts.

**Angle:** the outfit is incidental to a moment of the life being built — style as evidence of the life, not the subject of the post.

**Representative hook (on-screen text, first 1–2 seconds):**
> "A Saturday in the life I'm building."
**(Inference)**: ties directly to the brief's central identity — "A woman engineering her own life" **(Evidence — brief, line 12)** — and avoids overlap with Tue/Wed's more style-decision-focused angle, keeping the four touchpoints distinct rather than repetitive.

**Caption direction:**
> Documentary-style narration of the day (café, Cape Town, single-mother-building beats woven in per brief's pillar list, line 18), with the affiliate mention arriving naturally and once: "Outfit's linked in bio." No discount language, no urgency. **(Evidence — brief, lines 34, 35)**

### Sunday — Rest/Review Slot (affiliate tracking folded in, never a separate affiliate day)

**Function per brief:** this is the *only* place affiliate tracking/recap is allowed to surface explicitly, and it must stay inside the existing rest/review format. **(Evidence — brief, line 40)**: "Fold affiliate tracking into the existing Sunday rest/review slot — never a separate 'affiliate day.'"

**Angle:** a quiet, personal week-in-review — the affiliate/"what's in rotation" recap is one beat inside a broader rest/reflection post, not the headline.

**Representative hook (on-screen text over her face, unstyled/rest visual, still per line 36 — her face, not a graphic):**
> "What actually stayed in rotation this week."
**(Inference)**: reuses the brief's sanctioned "in rotation" phrasing **(line 34)**, framed as review/reflection (matching "rest/review slot" naming) rather than promotion — keeps this from reading as Susu's version of a "haul recap."

**Structure (Inference, built to satisfy line 39's no-results-before-90-days rule):**
1. Opening: general reflection on the week (documentary/personal tone, not a sales recap).
2. Middle: soft mention of what's in rotation, one link reference max, folded into the sentence rather than flagged as a CTA. **(Evidence — brief, line 35)**
3. Close: no performance claims, no "this worked," no numbers — because the brief requires no results judgment before 90 days and prioritizes saves/shares over likes. **(Evidence — brief, line 39)**: the copy should explicitly invite saving ("saving this for later") rather than liking, since that is the stated success signal.

**Do-not list for this slot (explicit, since this is the highest-risk slot for drifting into "affiliate day" territory):**
- No "shop my week" framing.
- No multiple links or a link list.
- No metrics, growth numbers, or "sales are picking up" language — none exist in the brief and Phase 1/Phase 2 both flagged this as a fabrication risk. **(Evidence — Phase 2, Proof Requirements section)**

## Braise Beauty BTS — Angle Within Its Own Pillar

**Function per brief:** Braise Beauty is its own weekly pillar, run behind-the-scenes and built publicly — **not** folded into the Tue/Wed/Sat/Sun fashion-affiliate touchpoints, and not a launch or sales pitch since no sales mechanism exists yet. **(Evidence — brief, lines 18, 21)**; **(Evidence — Phase 2, Core Offer #2 and handoff notes)**: "angle it as founding-stage documentation, never as a launch/sales pitch."

**Angle:** the absence of a finished product is the content, not a gap to hide. Founding-stage decisions (formulation, naming, packaging) are the proof material.
**(Inference, extending Phase 2's mechanism note)**: this is a direct extension of "built publicly" **(line 18)** — not stated verbatim in the brief but a low-risk inference from it, consistent with how Phase 2 already framed the mechanism.

**Representative hook (on-screen text over her face, BTS setting — never a product-shot slide, per line 36):**
> "What 'founding stage' actually looks like."
**(Inference)**: names the honesty explicitly rather than performing polish, matching the brief's "honest" and "documentary" voice descriptors **(Evidence — brief, line 24)**.

**Content beats for this pillar (Inference, grounded in brief facts only):**
- Formulation/testing footage on her own skin — directly answers the secondary audience's stated need: "women with deeper skin tones looking for beauty products that celebrate their complexion." **(Evidence — brief, line 7)**
- Naming/branding decisions shown as engineering-style problem-solving, reinforcing the "systems mindset" credibility source used across all pillars. **(Evidence — brief, line 24)**
- No "coming soon" urgency language, no waitlist count, no preorder pressure — the brief names no such mechanism, and Phase 1 flagged fabricated demand signals as a risk to avoid. **(Evidence — Phase 2, Objection Map row 6; Phase 1 flag inherited)**

**Explicit exclusion:** no CTA to "buy," "preorder," or "join the list" anywhere in this pillar's content — the brief's offer architecture places Braise Beauty at "witness the build," not "purchase," at this stage. **(Evidence — Phase 2, Core Offer #2)**

## Cross-Touchpoint Distinctiveness Check (why these four angles don't repeat each other)

| Slot | Core angle | Distinct from others because |
|---|---|---|
| Tue outfit Reel | Style as decision-system | Single-outfit, function-first framing |
| Wed styling carousel | Styling as decision *logic* shown step-by-step | Only slot using multi-slide "why" breakdown |
| Sat outfit/lifestyle Reel | Style as evidence of the life being built | Only slot crossing into Documentary pillar identity |
| Sun rest/review | Quiet week reflection + folded-in rotation recap | Only slot where affiliate tracking is explicitly named, per line 40 |
| Braise Beauty BTS | Founding-stage honesty as the product | Only pillar with zero affiliate/sales framing at all |

**(Inference)**: this differentiation check is not brief-stated but directly follows from the brief's instruction that affiliate content must never feel like a single repeated promo lane (line 18) — five near-identical "shop this" posts across the week would violate that instruction even if each individual post complied with the language rules.
