# Offer Architecture — Phase 2 (grounded in business-brief.md only)

Methodology note: every claim below is labeled **(Evidence — brief, line X)** with paraphrase/quote, or **(Inference)** with reasoning shown. No external data, no fabricated performance numbers. Per Phase 1, WTP and Searchability were scored low precisely because the brief contains no conversion/sales/waitlist data — that gap is preserved here, not papered over.

Scope discipline (per task instruction): this architecture covers only the two currently-real revenue lines — (1) affiliate income already live inside the Fashion & Outfits pillar, and (2) Braise Beauty founding-stage demand-building. ## Offer Architecture

### Positioning

**"I help ambitious women elevate their style and their life — because when you look good, you believe bigger — not by performing an aesthetic, but by documenting the real build of Braise Beauty and an engineered life."**
**(Evidence — brief, line 10)**: this is the brief's own core promise, quoted near-verbatim. I did not invent a new positioning line; the brief already states the sharpest possible version of this.

Adapted offer-formula read-through (Inference, mapping the brief onto the standard formula since this is a personal-brand/content business, not a software product — forcing "AI-powered system" language here would itself violate the brief's "no generic AI-sounding output" constraint, line 41):

*I help ambitious women (25–40) elevate their style and their life without abandoning the 9–5 or performing struggle, by running a weekly five-pillar documentary build-log (Documentary, Fashion, Mindset, Single-Mother-Building, Braise Beauty BTS) that turns consistent, story-first content into affiliate income now and Braise Beauty demand as the brand launches.*
**(Evidence — brief, lines 4, 14, 17-18, 21, 27-31)** for audience, content goal, pillars, offer-path sequencing, and the narrative rules that explicitly forbid an escape/struggle framing.

### Core Offer

Two live monetization surfaces — treated as parallel, not sequential, since Phase 1 explicitly flagged that conflating them or treating a third (gated) line as active would misrepresent the brief:

**1. Affiliate income (near-term, already active).**
**(Evidence — brief, line 21)**: "affiliate commission from fashion/styling content, already integrated into the existing weekly schedule (Tue outfit Reel, Wed styling carousel, Sat outfit/lifestyle Reel)." This is not a new mechanism to design — it is an existing operational line that this architecture must optimize inside its current real estate, not expand.

**2. Braise Beauty founding-stage demand-building (emerging, not yet transactional).**
**(Evidence — brief, line 21)**: "Emerging anchor offer: Braise Beauty product line (lip oil, liner, blush compact) as it moves out of founding stage." **(Evidence — brief, line 18)**: delivered via the "Braise Beauty (behind-the-scenes, built publicly)" pillar. There is no sales, preorder, or waitlist mechanism named in the brief — so the "offer" at this stage is not yet "buy," it is "witness the build," which functions as demand pre-loading.

Excluded from Core Offer: paid guide/capsule-wardrobe system. **(Evidence — brief, line 21)**: "Longer-term possibility: a paid guide/offer... once the documentary + fashion content has proven demand." Per Phase 1's explicit flag, this is gated behind proof that does not yet exist in the brief and is intentionally omitted from the active offer below.

### Unique Mechanism

**Name (Inference — proposed, not brief-stated): "The Build Log."**
Reasoning: the brief's documentary pillar is literally titled "Building Her" **(Evidence — brief, line 18)**, and the central identity is "A woman engineering her own life" **(Evidence — brief, line 12)**. The mechanism that makes the promise believable is not a sales funnel — it's the visible, cumulative weekly record itself.

How it works, grounded entirely in stated structure:
- Five pillars run weekly, and monetized content is never isolated in its own promotional lane. **(Evidence — brief, line 18)**: "affiliate links and Braise Beauty are introduced through story, never as an ad or a hard sell."
- Trust compounds through repetition of the same four fixed touchpoints (Tue/Wed/Sat/Sun) rather than through increased sales pressure. **(Evidence — brief, line 21, 40)**.
- The "engineer's systems mindset" is the credibility engine — she is not claiming beauty-influencer authority, she is applying the same discipline she uses professionally (automation engineer, **line 4**) to style and brand-building, which is a distinct and defensible credibility source. **(Evidence — brief, lines 4, 24)**.
- Because Braise Beauty is explicitly "founding stage" **(Evidence — brief, line 4)**, the mechanism converts an ordinary limitation (no product yet) into content: the *absence* of a finished product is reframed as the proof material itself (BTS of formulation/branding decisions). **(Inference)**: this is the standard "build in public" logic applied to the brief's stated facts — not stated outright in the brief, but a direct extension of "built publicly" (line 18).

### Value Ladder

Adapted from the standard four-rung formula to the two rungs the brief actually supports, plus the honest gap where a lead-magnet/gated rung is not stated in the brief and must be flagged as such.

1. **Free — weekly five-pillar content.** **(Evidence — brief, lines 17-18)**: Documentary, Fashion & outfits (affiliate embedded), Mindset, Single-mother building, Braise Beauty BTS. This is the entire top of funnel; the brief describes no separate free lead-magnet asset (no email opt-in, downloadable, etc. is mentioned anywhere in lines 1-42).

2. **Micro-conversion — bio/stories link (the closest thing to a "lead magnet" this brief supports).** **(Evidence — brief, line 35)**: "Link goes in bio/stories, mentioned once at most in caption." **(Evidence — brief, line 40)**: Sunday's existing rest/review slot is where affiliate tracking already lives — no new day is created. **(Inference — flagged as proposal, not brief-stated)**: this Sunday slot could double as a soft "what's in rotation this week" recap that reinforces the bio link, but this must stay inside the existing slot, not become a new promotional format, per the brief's explicit constraint against a separate "affiliate day" (line 40).

3. **Monetizable rung, active now — two parallel lines, not sequential:**
   - Affiliate commission via Tue/Wed/Sat content. **(Evidence — brief, line 21)**.
   - Braise Beauty demand-building via BTS pillar, with the product line itself (lip oil, liner, blush compact) as the eventual purchasable product once it exits founding stage. **(Evidence — brief, line 21)**.

4. **Gated / future rung — NOT active, listed only per brief's own sequencing:** a paid guide or "elevate your style" system (e.g., capsule wardrobe). **(Evidence — brief, line 21)**: explicitly conditioned on "once the documentary + fashion content has proven demand" — and per Phase 1, no such proof (sales, conversion, engagement-to-revenue data) currently exists in the brief. This rung should not appear in any near-term content plan or CTA.

### Proof Requirements

What must be shown on screen, and — equally important — what must be honestly *not* claimed, per the brief's own rules:

- **Her face as the visual background at all times, never plain colour slides or generic text carousels.** **(Evidence — brief, line 36)**. This is itself a proof mechanic: the audience is meant to see the person, not a graphic, reinforcing "documented" over "performed."
- **Centred, thin serif text only, with thin divider lines, no border frames.** **(Evidence — brief, line 37)**. Visual consistency across weeks functions as proof of the "engineered/systemized" premise — an inconsistent visual system would contradict the "systems mindset" claim (line 24).
- **Real behind-the-scenes material for Braise Beauty** (formulation decisions, packaging, naming — whatever is actually happening in the founding stage), not polished ad-style product shots. **(Evidence — brief, line 18)**: "behind-the-scenes, built publicly."
- **No results claimed or judged before 90 days; saves and shares are the stated success signal, not likes.** **(Evidence — brief, line 39)**. This means the content itself should never show or imply revenue screenshots, growth charts, or "this worked" framing inside the first 90 days — the proof requirement here is patience made visible, not hidden.
- **Explicit gap, not filled:** the brief contains zero sales figures, conversion rates, waitlist numbers, or follower-growth data for Braise Beauty. **(Evidence — Phase 1 output)**: WTP and Searchability were scored low "specifically because the brief contains no performance/conversion/market data — flagged, not fabricated." This architecture inherits that same flag rather than inventing placeholder numbers.
